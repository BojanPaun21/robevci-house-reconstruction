# robevci-house-reconstruction
Interactive 3D desktop reconstruction of the interior of the Robevci House built in Autodesk Maya and Unreal Engine. Bachelor thesis project, ISVMA.

A desktop application that lets users walk through a 3D model of the interior of the Robevci House in Ohrid, North Macedonia, and read information about selected artifacts. Built in Autodesk Maya and Unreal Engine 4.27 as part of a bachelor thesis at ISVMA (University of Information Science and Technology “St. Paul the Apostle”).
